Imposter EDU – QCAA

Select subject(s), IA and difficulty to generate a curriculum-aligned secret word for your Imposter game. One random player is the Imposter.

QCAA-aligned (Year 12 style) Subjects + IA + difficulty Pass & play on one device

1. Game Settings

Subjects (choose one or more)
IA (content unit)
Difficulty
Players (4+)
4 players
Hint mode

2. Pass & Play

1) Choose subjects, IA, difficulty and player names.
2) Click Generate Word & Start Round.
3) A secret word is chosen; one random player is the Imposter.
4) Pass the device: each player taps to see their card.
– Non-imposters see: Subject + Word.
– Imposter sees: “You are the Imposter” and, if hints are on, the subject only.
