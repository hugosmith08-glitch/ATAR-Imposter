2. Pass & Play

1) Choose subjects, IA, difficulty and players.
2) Click Generate Word & Start Round.
3) A secret word is chosen and one random player is the imposter.
4) Pass the device. Each player taps to see their role:
– Most players see the word.
– One player sees IMPOSTER (with an optional subject hint).
